# Partner Program — operating spec

Referral partners (agencies, advisors, operators in adjacent trades)
introduce client businesses. Gavari sells and delivers under its own brand.
Partners earn **20% of referred revenue**.

## Lifecycle, as the system runs it

1. **Source.** The Radar scores partner candidates the same way it scores
   prospects: public signals, evidence attached.
2. **Invite.** Personal outreach from the operator — never a blast. The
   first conversation offers a free audit of the partner's own business or
   a client's, so the product is experienced before it is referred.
3. **Onboard.** Partner gets: the one-sheet (what Gavari installs, who it
   fits, what it costs), a named contact, and a simple rule — introduce,
   Gavari handles the rest.
4. **Track.** One register: partner, introductions, status, revenue,
   share owed. Statuses: joined → first introduction → first closed client
   → producing.
5. **Ping the milestones.** The system flags the hour a partner's first
   introduction lands and the day their first client closes, so the
   operator follows up the same day, while it matters.
6. **Report.** A short weekly rollup to the operator: joined, active,
   producing, share payable. Partners hear their numbers from the operator,
   not from a portal.

## Rules

- Percentage is agreed in the first conversation and never renegotiated
  downward after an introduction is made.
- A live conversation with a referred business pauses all automated
  follow-up to that business across every channel.
- Do-not-contact is global: one opt-out ends every lane, for prospects and
  partners alike.
